# Verification

ArchiveBridge is currently an MVP under local development. Unit/integration tests and synthetic fixtures are included; they do not establish production qualification. Windows/Linux distribution evidence, package identities, browser flows, interruption recovery and independent findings will be recorded for each published version.

`verify` checks exported bytes and structure against the unsigned manifest. It does not authenticate the manifest or compare a full Google account. The initial MVP does not claim forced-termination recovery or a completed Immich adapter. Supported scope and limits are in [support](support.md).

Release packages will include SHA-256 checksums and a versioned evidence report binding the exact source, platform packages, real acceptance results and known limitations. No pilot, download count or broad real-user success is claimed.
